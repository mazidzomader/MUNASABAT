# AI Usage Statement

**Project:** Munasabat: Wedding Planner and Guest Management Platform  
**Developer:** Abdullah Al Mazid Zomader (ID: 24241189)  

Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools were utilized strictly in a targeted capacity to assist with front-end UI scaffolding, layout drafting, and styling boilerplate across Flutter widgets. In contrast, the application's overall system architecture, four-layer design pattern, and Riverpod state management workflows were conceived and engineered entirely by the developer. All core business logic, including Firestore NoSQL schema design, guest join-request and check-in workflows, budget calculations, and Base64 image processing, was implemented independently without AI code generation. Furthermore, the Stripe sandbox payment integration and granular Firestore security rules were authored directly by the developer to enforce data integrity and platform access control. Overall, AI served solely as a productivity aid for routine interface construction, while all architectural decisions and intellectual logic remain the original work of the author.
